## My Reflection

The AI coding agent significantly accelerated the drafting of our engineering contract for Lab 2. What would have taken me several days of careful writing was condensed into focused prompt-response cycles where I provided the stakeholder requirements and the AI generated structured drafts of the specification, API contract, UI specification, and test plan. The AI was particularly effective at identifying edge cases I might have overlooked, such as the inactive-requester 403 path and the safe filename behavior for attachment storage.

However, I want to emphasize that I retained full responsibility for every business rule, API contract, and test case in these documents. The AI drafts were starting points that I reviewed, corrected, and approved before inclusion. For example, I corrected the attachment MIME allowlist to match the exact 4 types specified in the labsheet, ensured the ownership enforcement was at the data-access layer rather than just the UI, and verified that every acceptance criteria had at least one automated test. The AI occasionally suggested broader implementations than required, and I constrained the scope to match the labsheet exactly.

In conclusion, the AI served as an efficient drafting partner, but the final engineering contract reflects my understanding of the requirements and my judgment about what constitutes a correct implementation. Every document has been reviewed for internal consistency across all four files, ensuring that the specification, API contract, UI specification, and test plan form a cohesive, traceable, and implementable engineering contract that strictly adheres to the stakeholder handout.
