## My Reflection

AI sped up contract drafting, services, and boilerplate UI/tests, but I owned the contract. I enforced frozen FR/BR/AC/AD numbering, session-cookie plus CSRF over JWT, view-only Admin, requester-self identity, masked-404 byte-equality, one-signal-per-episode, copy-on-claim timing, and the 17-transition BR-15 matrix through failing tests first, and caught real bugs (FK-style id divergence, owner-status guard leaks, spoofable actor field, inverted toggles, pagination/ILIKE edge cases) through reproduction. I let AI generate, I verified against the labsheet and merged only after green gates.
